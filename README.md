# Kastel World

**A grounded speculative world linking Kastel comics, global node canon, real-world resilience technology, practical field manuals and open community builds.**

Kastel World is the story-and-reality layer of the wider Kastel project.

Its central pathway is:

```text
COMIC
  ↓
WORLD
  ↓
VISION
  ↓
FIELD MANUAL
  ↓
BUILDS
  ↓
COMMUNITY
  ↓
REAL PROJECTS
```

The comic is the imaginative front door. The Atlas fixes the fictional world underneath it. The Field Manual explains real technologies behind selected fictional systems. Builds turn those explanations into practical projects. Community testing can improve the Commons and feed new ideas back into the fiction.

> **Fiction is the laboratory.**  
> **The Commons is the bridge.**  
> **Reality is the test.**

## What this repository is

Kastel World contains five linked layers:

1. **Comic** — recurring adventures, characters and missions.
2. **Canon** — the fixed political, technological and social rules of the fictional world.
3. **Atlas** — places, nodes, terrain, buildings, routes and persistent story state.
4. **Field Manual** — real-world technologies and practices corresponding to elements seen in the comic.
5. **Builds & Community** — practical projects, open designs, build logs and tested improvements.

The project is designed so that a reader can enjoy the comic without reading any technical material, while a technically curious reader can follow a marker such as `TECH-014`, `NODE-SA-01` or `BUILD-005` into progressively deeper layers.

## Story premise

Kastel World imagines a near future in which much of everyday life runs through an increasingly integrated **Blue Rail**: AI-mediated platforms, identity systems, financial rails, corporate cloud infrastructure, automated governance, security systems and optimisation machinery.

Alongside it is a distributed **Red Rail** of human-scale Kastel nodes. These communities do not reject the wider world. They use mainstream systems where useful while maintaining enough independent infrastructure, knowledge, productive capacity, income and relationships to preserve meaningful agency and graceful degradation.

The world is deliberately not a simple story of heroic rebels versus an evil state. It is a world of mixed incentives, dependencies, compromise, humour, trade, friendship, technology, bureaucracy, disasters, local cultures and competing models of how people should live.

## Reality status

Every technology entry should carry one of four labels:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **REAL NOW** | Readily achievable with currently available technology. |
| **EXPERIMENTAL** | Exists, but remains specialised, immature, expensive or technically demanding. |
| **PROJECTED** | A plausible near-future extension of current technology. |
| **FICTIONAL** | Created primarily for the story. |

The project should never quietly present fiction as current technical reality.

## Practical-link principle

Where a comic scene uses technology with a real-world analogue, the website should link to a concise entry containing:

- purpose;
- reality status;
- current analogue;
- approximate cost band;
- difficulty;
- whether internet access is required;
- whether the system can be self-hosted;
- whether professional installation is advisable or legally required;
- primary-source links to official documentation;
- relevant Kastel Build;
- comic appearances;
- limitations and safety notes.

Prefer original projects, manufacturers, standards bodies and public authorities over affiliate links and SEO summaries.

## Relationship to the wider Kastel organisation

This repository has a different job from the other Kastel repositories.

- **`kastel-ground-truth`** — canonical Kastel doctrine and design principles.
- **`kastel-stack`** — adaptive organism OS for independent businesses.
- **`kastel-node-template`** — starter structure for private business-node deployments.
- **`power_politics`** — power and strategic theory.
- **`kastel-world`** — speculative world, comic, Atlas, Field Manual, Builds and the public story-to-reality bridge.

Kastel World may draw on the wider Kastel philosophy, but it should not silently redefine canonical doctrine.

## First canonical prototype

The first detailed physical node is provisionally the **South African Garden Node**, inspired by the inland Garden Route / Outeniqua–Klein Karoo transition.

It provides a rich test bed because a single node can naturally combine:

- resilient computing;
- local AI;
- solar and storage;
- water autonomy;
- wildfire and flood awareness;
- local communications;
- workshop fabrication;
- permaculture and greenhouse systems;
- rotating residents;
- business and trading income;
- regional travel;
- community relationships;
- graceful degradation during infrastructure failures.

It is a fictional site informed by real geography and technologies. Exact real properties, residents and sensitive security arrangements should not be published as fictional operational targets.

## Safety and ethical boundary

Kastel World is about **agency, resilience, knowledge, repair, preparedness, peaceful cooperation and lawful parallel capacity**.

The practical layer does not publish instructions for weaponisation, offensive cyber operations, sabotage, covert political operations, violent confrontation or harmful autonomous systems. Security material should emphasise detection, deterrence, delay, safe refuge, verified response, fire safety, communications resilience and cooperation with legitimate emergency or security services.
