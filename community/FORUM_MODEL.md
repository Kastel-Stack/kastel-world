# Forum Model

## Initial recommendation

Do not build a custom forum first.

Start with a low-maintenance platform such as GitHub Discussions or an established forum product.

## Proposed categories

### Field Manual
- Local AI & compute
- Home lab
- Communications
- Mapping & GIS
- Home automation

### Land & resilience
- Permaculture
- Water
- Wildfire
- Flood
- Off-grid energy
- Environmental sensing

### Workshop
- Electronics
- 3D printing
- Wood/metal
- Repair
- Open hardware

### World
- Comic
- Characters
- Nodes
- Continuity
- Art pipeline

### Builds
- Build logs
- Tested variants
- Troubleshooting

## Trust labels

Community posts should be able to distinguish:

- idea;
- untested build;
- tested by author;
- replicated;
- professionally reviewed.

Do not collapse popularity into evidence.

## Moderation

Remove:

- doxxing;
- private-site exposure;
- violent operational planning;
- malicious cyber content;
- scams;
- undisclosed affiliate spam;
- harassment.

Strong criticism of ideas is allowed. Personal intimidation is not.
