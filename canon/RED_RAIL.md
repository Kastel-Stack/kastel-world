# Red Rail

## Definition

The Red Rail is the autonomy-building layer of Kastel World.

It consists of infrastructure, skills, institutions, relationships and livelihoods that remain usable without total dependence on the Blue Rail.

## Typical capabilities

- self-hosted compute and storage;
- local AI;
- open-source software;
- independent archives;
- resilient networking;
- satellite and mesh communications;
- repair workshops;
- local production;
- food growing;
- water management;
- portable businesses;
- trusted professional networks;
- education and skill transfer;
- human-scale governance.

## Not isolation

Red Rail characters still:

- pay taxes;
- use airports;
- operate companies;
- use mainstream banks;
- buy commercial hardware;
- attend universities;
- use cloud AI;
- sell to Blue Rail customers.

The key discipline is **modularity and exit**.

A node asks:

> What can we use safely?  
> What must we own?  
> What must remain portable?  
> What is a single point of failure?

## Economic realism

Nodes require income.

Possible livelihoods include:

- software;
- remote professional services;
- research;
- design;
- specialist manufacturing;
- trading;
- agriculture;
- tourism;
- education;
- repair;
- media;
- consultancy;
- local services.

Red Rail resilience without sustainable income is not canonical resilience.

## Social realism

Nodes can fail through:

- burnout;
- bad governance;
- romance;
- ego;
- poor accounting;
- conflict;
- skills loss;
- complacency;
- environmental shocks.

Human factors should generate as many plots as technology does.
