# SA-01 Location Anchor

**Site ID:** `SITE-SA-01`  
**Status:** canonical fictional anchor v0.2  
**Canonical coordinate:** **-33.8115, 22.4650**  
**Region inspiration:** foothills north of Herold, Western Cape, South Africa  
**Exact real parcel:** none; this is a fictional worldbuilding coordinate

## Why this anchor

The canonical site is intentionally positioned between:

- **Herold**, the nearby rural hamlet; and
- the **Du Toits–Langkloof paragliding launch**, a real inland flying site north of Herold.

This gives NODE-SA-01 a plausible spatial relationship to:

- an inland Garden Route / Outeniqua landscape;
- a thermal / cross-country paragliding ridge;
- rural roads and nearby settlements;
- George and Garden Route services to the south;
- the drier interior to the north.

The coordinate is used to fix the geography of the fictional world. It is not a claim that the point is a purchasable, buildable or suitable real parcel.

## Flying-site relationship

Reference launch:

- Du Toits–Langkloof launch: approximately **-33.7800, 22.4800**

The fictional house anchor lies a few kilometres south-west of the launch in straight-line terms, giving the story-world node easy conceptual access to the flying ridge while remaining closer to Herold and ordinary road access.

Actual pilot access, permissions, airspace, weather, landing options and launch rules remain governed by the real site operator and aviation authorities.

## Production use

Use this coordinate to anchor:
