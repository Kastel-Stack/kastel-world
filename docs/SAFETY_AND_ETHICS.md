# Safety and Ethics

## Purpose

Kastel World depicts a future shaped by concentrated technological and institutional power, but its practical project is constructive rather than militant.

## Practical-layer principles

The Field Manual and Builds may support:

- resilience;
- repair;
- emergency preparedness;
- local computing;
- privacy;
- communications redundancy;
- environmental monitoring;
- food growing;
- water management;
- safe building design;
- lawful security;
- community mutual aid.

They should not provide operational guidance for:

- weaponisation;
- offensive cyber activity;
- sabotage;
- violent confrontation;
- clandestine political operations;
- evasion of legitimate emergency response;
- autonomous harmful systems.

## Security

Security content should use the hierarchy:

```text
detect
→ deter
→ delay
→ shelter/refuge
→ communicate
→ obtain legitimate response
→ recover
```

## AI

AI may:

- summarise;
- classify;
- detect anomalies;
- search documents;
- assist diagnosis;
- draft recommendations.

For high-consequence systems, deterministic controls and human judgement remain authoritative.

## Community

Kastel should not turn difference of opinion into purity tests. The world is explicitly pluralist and should contain disagreement within Red Rail communities as well as within Blue Rail institutions.

## Environmental stewardship

"Autonomy" does not mean externalising damage. Water diversion, firebreaks, land use, radio systems, energy installations and buildings must respect ecological, neighbour and legal constraints.

## Story ethics

The fiction may contain coercion, violence or morally compromised characters. Depiction is not practical endorsement. The real-world layer should keep the distinction explicit.
