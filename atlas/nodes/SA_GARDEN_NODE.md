# NODE-SA-01 — South African Garden Node

**Status:** canonical prototype, v0.1  
**Region inspiration:** inland from South Africa's Garden Route, near the Outeniqua / Klein Karoo transition  
**Exact location:** fictional  
**Canonical map anchor:** `-33.8115, 22.4650` — a notional foothill site north of Herold, chosen to place the node within easy conceptual reach of the Du Toits–Langkloof flying ridge. See [location anchor](../../spatial/nodes/SA01_LOCATION_ANCHOR.md).

## Narrative function

NODE-SA-01 is the first fully modelled Kastel node.

It is intended to demonstrate how a Red Rail community can remain connected to ordinary economic life while maintaining meaningful local capability.

The node is not a fortress or survivalist compound. It is a comfortable, productive, technologically sophisticated rural base.

## Canonical site package

The node now has three first-pass spatial specifications:

- [House K1](../../spatial/nodes/SA01_HOUSE_K1_SPEC.md) — compact two-bedroom modular autonomous house with study/control room.
- [Workshop / Lab W1](../../spatial/nodes/SA01_WORKSHOP_LAB_SPEC.md) — detached fabrication, electronics, repair and compute building.
- [Site layout](../../spatial/nodes/SA01_SITE_LAYOUT.md) — relationship between house, workshop, productive land, greenhouse, water and access.

These are comic-production canon, not construction documents.

## Residency model

The node is designed to remain viable for several weeks with automation and periodic inspection.

Longer absences favour rotating stewardship.

The system should support:

```text
automation handles routine operation
human residents handle exceptions
the knowledge base preserves institutional memory
```

Potential residents include owners, trusted friends, visiting researchers, remote workers, land stewards and collaborators.

## Economic base

Plausible Blue Rail income may include:

- remote professional work;
- software;
- digital products;
- trading/research;
- consulting;
- education;
- design;
- selective local production.

The node may also produce local goods, but should not be written as economically self-sufficient.
